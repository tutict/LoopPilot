## Repeated Across EXP-005 and EXP-006

- A red baseline requires attribution before a change is called regressive.
- A passing command proves only its selected tests; discovery and prerequisite gaps
  remain material.
- Unsupported Worker narratives require direct verification before they update
  authoritative state.
- Tool, service, and environment failures remain Execution Infrastructure
  Incidents unless evidence separately proves a product or protocol defect.
